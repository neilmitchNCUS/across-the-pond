Across the Pond Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2026

Across the Pond is a UK and US conversion and reference app for iPhone. This privacy policy explains how the app handles user information.

Data Collection

Across the Pond does not collect personal data.

The app does not require an account, does not use advertising, does not use tracking, and does not send user data to a server.

Favorites

Favorites are stored locally on your device. They are not transmitted to the developer or to any external service.

Email Support

If you contact support by email, your email address and message will be used only to respond to your request.

Children's Privacy

Across the Pond does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
